What to disenchant for each material

These ranges are the Classic ones (level of the green item you disenchant), which Forever builds on. Blue items give shards instead.

Magic Essence, Strange Dust

Green items lvl 5 to 20

Everything you loot in the starting zones, The Barrens and Stonetalon Mountains.

Astral Essence, Soul Dust

Green items lvl 21 to 35

Ashenvale, Thousand Needles, Hillsbrad Foothills, Desolace.

Mystic Essence, Vision Dust

Green items lvl 31 to 45

Arathi Highlands, Stranglethorn Vale, Badlands, Feralas.

Nether Essence

Green items lvl 41 to 50

Tanaris, The Hinterlands, Feralas, Searing Gorge.

Large Radiant Shard

Green items lvl 46 to 50

Un'Goro, Felwood, Burning Steppes, and level 45-50 dungeons.

Tip: when prices are low, buying green items on the AH to disenchant is often cheaper than buying the essences.

Forever specifics

  • Enchanted Lute at 20: raises armor, stats and resistances of nearby players.
  • Arcane Reclaimer at 140: improves what you get from disenchanting.
  • Arcane Forge at 300: required for some recipes.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • New formulas are sold by the Azeroth Commerce Authority, including off-hand items (totems, librams, orbs).
  • Announced at BlizzCon but not yet confirmed: enchanters could enchant their own rings.
  • The profession has 219 recipes, 63 of them new.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever Enchanting guide for the path and trainers · wow.gg for the new formulas.

Steps above 225 weren't playable in the beta: they're marked "to verify". Rod materials are inferred from the guide's totals: check the recipe at the trainer before buying. Forever-only names are translated from the French client and may differ slightly in game.
