Forever specifics

  • Announced at BlizzCon, not yet confirmed: Wild Beasts (+5% damage against beasts and whelps) and better odds of rare hides.
  • Pristine Leather: new Forever component, keep it for Leatherworking.
  • Camp Chair (skinners' camp item): +2% crit according to Method.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • The ideal levelling combo for leather and mail classes: Skinning + Leatherworking.
