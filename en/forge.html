Forever specifics

  • Grindstone at 20: raises the Strength of players at camp.
  • Anvil at 140: required for some recipes, at camp.
  • Master Forge at 300: required for some recipes.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Specialisations: Armorsmith or Weaponsmith, then Master Hammersmith, Axesmith or Swordsmith. You pick only one, and their recipes aren't in this path.
  • Six mail recipes (Invulnerable Mail, Helm of the Great Chief, Stormshroud Gauntlets…) moved to Leatherworking.
  • Announced at BlizzCon but not yet confirmed: Full Repair (repairs all your gear, on a cooldown) and belt buckles that add stats.
  • The profession has 431 recipes: 197 new and 181 changed.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever Blacksmithing guide · Kami Labs, WoW Forever professions.

Steps above 225 weren't playable in the beta: they're marked "to verify". If a recipe vendor belongs to the other faction, use the auction house. Forever-only names are translated from the French client and may differ slightly in game.
