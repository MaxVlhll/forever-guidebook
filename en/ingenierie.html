Forever specifics

  • Parts Vendor Bot at 20: sells components at camp.
  • Repair Bot at 140: sells components and repairs your gear.
  • Anarchist's Workbench at 300: required for some recipes.
  • Gnomish or Goblin specialisation: a quest makes you pick a school, each with its own exclusive items. Their recipes aren't in this path.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Rough Dynamite now costs only one Rough Blasting Powder. Flash Bomb is learned from a quest.
  • The profession has 242 recipes: 78 new and 32 changed. According to Kami Labs, Engineering is mostly a PvP pick: no engineering item appears in PvE BiS lists.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever Engineering guide · Overgear, Engineering guide.

Steps above 225 weren't playable in the beta: they're marked "to verify". If a recipe vendor belongs to the other faction, use the auction house. The guide mentions no tool (wrench, hammer): if your client asks for one, craft it before that step. Forever-only names are translated from the French client and may differ slightly in game.
