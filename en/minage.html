Forever specifics

  • Announced at BlizzCon, not yet confirmed: Made of Metal (+5% total health), extra ore from veins and a chance at rare or combat materials.
  • Lodestone (miners' camp item): melee attack power bonus, according to Method.
  • Forever has 38 node types: 2 new (Depleted Copper Vein, Heavy Thorium Vein) and 5 changed.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Pairs well with Blacksmithing or Engineering, which eat hundreds of bars.
