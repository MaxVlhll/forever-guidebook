Where to get each cloth

Cloth drops from humanoids. The level ranges below are the Classic ones, which Forever builds on.

Linen Cloth

Humanoids lvl 5 to 15

Starting zones, The Barrens (centaurs, quilboars), Silverpine Forest.

Wool Cloth

Humanoids lvl 15 to 25

Stonetalon Mountains, Ashenvale, Hillsbrad Foothills, Wailing Caverns.

Silk Cloth

Humanoids lvl 25 to 35

Thousand Needles, Desolace, Arathi Highlands, Scarlet Monastery.

Mageweave Cloth

Humanoids lvl 35 to 45

Stranglethorn Vale, Feralas, Tanaris, The Hinterlands, Zul'Farrak.

Runecloth

Humanoids lvl 48 to 60

Felwood (satyrs), Eastern and Western Plaguelands, Winterspring, level 55+ dungeons.

Keep all the cloth you loot while levelling: it's by far the biggest cost of this profession.

Forever specifics

  • Faction Banner at 20: raises the Spirit of nearby players.
  • Spinning Wheel at 140: crafts some materials at camp.
  • Loom at 300: required for some recipes.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Announced at BlizzCon but not yet confirmed: more cloth from enemies, and a cloak embroidery (attack power, spell power or Stamina).
  • The profession has 406 recipes, 180 of them new.
