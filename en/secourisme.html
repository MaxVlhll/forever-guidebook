Forever specifics

  • Healing potions (minor to major) moved from Alchemy to First Aid.
  • New heals: poultices (from 90), tourniquets (from 120) and improved anti-venoms.
  • First Aid Kit at 20: raises the Stamina of players at camp.
  • Toxin Study at 140: helps with healing potions and anti-venoms, at camp.
  • Plague Doctor's Lab at 300: required for some potions and poultices.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • The profession has 30 recipes, 17 of them new.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever First Aid guide · ConquestCapped, healing potions.

Steps above 225 weren't playable in the beta: they're marked "to verify". If a recipe vendor belongs to the other faction, use the auction house. Forever-only names are translated from the French client and may differ slightly in game.
