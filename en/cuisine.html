Forever specifics

  • Basic Campfire Kit at 20: your camp can hold 3 items.
  • Journeyman's Campfire Kit at 90: 5 items.
  • Specialist's Campfire Kit at 200: 10 items.
  • New teas and frappés (Tea of Serenity at 25, Royal Tea at 100, Sage's Tea at 250…) and a feast, Macaron's Feast at 140.
  • Some dishes give "Well Fed" and an experience bonus on mob kills (announced, not yet confirmed). Method mentions +5% experience for 15 minutes.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • The profession has 125 recipes, 44 of them new.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever Cooking guide · Method, shaman guide (experience bonus).

Steps above 225 weren't playable in the beta: they're marked "to verify". If a recipe vendor belongs to the other faction, use the auction house. Forever-only names are translated from the French client and may differ slightly in game.
