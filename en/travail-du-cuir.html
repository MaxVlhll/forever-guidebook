Where to skin each leather

Your Skinning has to keep up: below level 20 you need about (mob level − 10) × 10; above it, about level × 5.

Light leather and hide

Beasts lvl 10 to 22

Horde The Barrens (zhevras, thunder lizards, kodos south of the Crossroads), Stonetalon Mountains.Alliance Westfall, Loch Modan, Darkshore.

Medium leather and hide

Beasts lvl 20 to 30

Horde Stonetalon Mountains, Ashenvale, Thousand Needles, Hillsbrad Foothills.Alliance Wetlands, Redridge Mountains, Duskwood, Ashenvale.

Heavy leather and hide

Beasts lvl 30 to 40

Arathi Highlands, Desolace, northern Stranglethorn.

Thick Leather

Beasts lvl 35 to 45

Southern Stranglethorn, Feralas, Badlands, Dustwallow Marsh.

Rugged Leather

Beasts lvl 48 and up

Un'Goro Crater, Felwood, Winterspring, Burning Steppes.

Thick Scorpid Scales

Scorpids lvl 50 and up

Silithus, Blasted Lands.

Keep the Pristine Leather you find while skinning: it's a new Forever component used by many new recipes.

Forever specifics

  • Camp Tent at 20: grants rested experience (up to 5% of a level). It costs 5 Light Leather.
  • Tanning Rack at 140: crafts some materials at camp.
  • Sewing Machine at 300: required for some recipes.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Specialisations at Artisan: Dragonscale, Elemental or Tribal. You pick only one.
  • The profession has 510 recipes, 277 of them new. This path follows the best ones for levelling, not the best ones to wear.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever Leatherworking guide for the path and trainers · Seemeta for colour thresholds · Expcarry for Pristine Leather.
