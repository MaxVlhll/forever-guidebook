Levelling talents

Levelling builds 10 → 60 for all nine classes, Alliance and Horde, based on the latest guides (up to 7 November). Each talent shows its in-game icon so you can spot it fast in your tree. What was played in the beta is marked tested, the rest projected.

Read this first. The beta stopped at level 30: nobody has played talents 31 to 60 yet, Blizzard moved talents again on 2 November and announced new class tuning for 10 November. If a talent isn't clickable in your tree yet, take the next one on the list and come back to it later.

With the "Talented" legacy bonus, your points arrive up to 5 levels earlier: follow the Points column instead of the Level column.
