Forever specifics

  • Healing potions left Alchemy: First Aid makes them now.
  • Mana Well at 20: restores mana to nearby seated players (doesn't stack with Blessing of Wisdom).
  • Fermenter at 140: crafts some components at camp.
  • Alchemy Lab at 300: required for some recipes.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Announced at BlizzCon but not yet confirmed: elixirs and flasks the alchemist drinks would last twice as long and be 25% stronger.
  • The profession has 179 recipes, 72 of them new. This path follows the best ones for levelling, not the best ones to sell.

Sources: Kami Labs, WoW Forever Alchemy guide for the path, recipes and trainers · ConquestCapped for the healing potion move.
