Forever specifics

  • Announced at BlizzCon, not yet confirmed: Nature's Gift (resistance to all magic schools), better odds of rare lotuses and more herbs per node.
  • Forever has 48 node types: 2 new (Withered Peacebloom, Stunted Silverleaf) and 8 changed.
  • Black Lotus no longer gives skill-ups.
  • Legacy points at 150, 225 and 300.
  • Pairs well with Alchemy, and First Aid now also needs herbs for healing potions.
